Privacy Policy

UCAT Prep · Effective date: 16 July 2026 · Updated: 11 November 2026 (Android)

Developer: MD Shahariar Nazim Joy · Contact: dmjks120@gmail.com

Summary

UCAT Prep is an offline-first exam preparation app. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs. We do not sell personal data.

Data we process

  • On-device study data: practice answers, bookmarks, streaks, study plan preferences, and mock results are stored locally on your device using Apple frameworks (e.g. SwiftData / UserDefaults) on iPhone and iPad, or app-private storage (SharedPreferences) on Android.
  • Purchases: Subscription and lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple through StoreKit / App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google through Google Play Billing on Android. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Notifications (optional): If you allow notifications, a local daily reminder may be scheduled on your device. Reminder scheduling does not send your data to our servers.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate an account system. We do not upload your contacts, photos, microphone, or health data. The app does not include third-party analytics or ad networks.

Sharing

We do not share personal study data with third parties. Apple may process purchase-related information under Apple’s privacy policy as part of App Store transactions, and Google under the Google Privacy Policy as part of Google Play transactions. Read-aloud on Android uses your device’s text-to-speech engine.

Children

UCAT Prep is intended for students preparing for university admissions testing. It is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Reset study progress in Settings.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings, or on Android in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings or Android Settings → Apps → UCAT Prep → Notifications.
  • Delete the app to remove on-device study data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
